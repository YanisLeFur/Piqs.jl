PIQSolver.jl ​

PIQSolver.jl (Permutational Invariant Quantum Solver) is a Julia package to study the open-system dynamics of ensembles of identical two-level systems (spins, qubits, atoms, ...) that are invariant under permutations of the two-level systems.

The density matrix of two-level systems lives in a space of dimension . When the Hamiltonian and the dissipation (local and collective emission, pumping and dephasing) are symmetric under the exchange of any two systems, the dynamics can be written in the Dicke basis . The required space then grows only as , so ensembles of hundreds of two-level systems can be simulated exactly.

PIQSolver.jl is a Julia port of PIQS, originally developed in Python by Nathan Shammah and Shahnawaz Ahmed and now part of QuTiP. It is built on top of QuantumToolbox.jl: every operator, state and Liouvillian it returns is a QuantumObject, so you can pass it directly to mesolve, expect, ptrace, ...

Installation ​

julia
using Pkg
Pkg.add("PIQSolver")

PIQSolver.jl is meant to be used together with QuantumToolbox.jl:

julia
Pkg.add("QuantumToolbox")

Quick start ​

Superradiant decay of   initially excited two-level systems:

julia
using PIQSolver, QuantumToolbox

N = 10
jz = jspin(N, op="z")
jp, jm = jspin(N, op="+"), jspin(N, op="-")

system = Dicke(N, collective_emission=1.0)   # collective (superradiant) emission
L = liouvillian_dicke(system)                # Liouvillian in the Dicke basis

rho0 = dicke(N, N / 2, N / 2)                # |j = N/2, m = N/2⟩, all excited
tlist = LinRange(0, 2, 200)
sol = mesolve(L, rho0, tlist, e_ops=[jz, jp * jm])

sol.expect[2, :]                             # emitted light intensity ⟨J₊J₋⟩(t)

See Getting started for the main concepts and Examples for complete worked examples.

Features ​

  • Collective spin operators in the Dicke basis (jspin) and in the full uncoupled basis.

  • Local and collective emission, pumping and dephasing, with Liouvillians in the Dicke basis (Dicke, lindbladian, liouvillian_dicke).

  • Dicke states and arbitrary density matrices in the Dicke basis (dicke, dicke_basis).

  • A fast solver for diagonal problems based on the permutational invariant matrix (pisolve, Pim).

  • Tensor products of superoperators of different dimensions (super_tensor), for example to couple the spins to a cavity mode.

  • Collapse operators in the uncoupled basis (collapse_uncoupled) to benchmark against brute-force simulations.
